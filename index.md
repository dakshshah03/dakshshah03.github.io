## About Me

I am a first-year MS Computer Science student at New York University, focusing on 3D perception, reconstruction, and computer graphics. I recently graduated from UC Santa Cruz with a double major in Computer Science and Math Theory and Computation, advised by Professor [Razvan Marinescu](https://razvanmarinescu.com/) on sparse-view CT reconstruction and Professor [James Davis](https://users.soe.ucsc.edu/~davis/) for egocentric reconstruction on wearable hardware.
